Defining Moment at College

Que sera, sera—what will be, will be. College is a major reality check. The real defining moments are often the challenging ones: receiving a disappointing midterm grade, preparing all night for a difficult chemistry exam, or overcoming obstacles in group projects.

The campus felt huge and overwhelming at first. Everything changed when I found supportive friends and became involved in student activities. Those experiences helped me gain confidence, adapt to college life, and feel at home.
