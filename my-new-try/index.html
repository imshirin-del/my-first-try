Defining Moments at College

Que sera, sera—what will be, will be. College is a major reality check. The real defining moments are often the challenging ones: receiving a disappointing end semester grade or overcoming obstacles in group project submissions.

The campus felt huge and overwhelming at first. Things became easier when I found supportive and cheerful people around, and became involved in various activities. Those experiences helped me gain confidence and adapt to college life.
